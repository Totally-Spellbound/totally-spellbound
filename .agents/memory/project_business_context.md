Totally Spellbound is Alison's (Andrew's mum, age 65) independent crystal and spiritual products shop. Physical store on Altram Street, Sutton-in-Ashfield, UK. Under 50 products, £1-65 range. Wholesale + custom laser engraving and 3D printing (key competitive advantage).

Shop takings: £100-250/week. Online sales: £0 currently. 6-month goal: extra £100-200/week online. Previous website failed (1 sale in a year — traffic problem). eBay worked but fees killed it.

Family team: Andrew (tech), Aaron (initial Shopify setup), Kirsty (daughter, runs Facebook lives/socials), sons pay for Shopify. Alison max 30 mins/week on tech.

**Why:** Andrew is building the Shopify website for his mum. Not trying to become a monopoly — goal is comfortable income and financial breathing room. The website should mirror the in-store experience: knowledgeable, helpful, curated, never pushy.

**How to apply:** All design and feature decisions should reinforce indie/authentic positioning. Don't over-engineer or make it feel corporate. Showcase personalisation capabilities (laser engraving, 3D printing) as a differentiator. Always use £ not $. Docs repo at docs/ has a full growth plan, section-by-section guides, and agent context file.
